About Fulcrum

Built by people who were tired of running the enterprise across six disconnected tools.

Fulcrum started as an internal tool at a logistics company that couldn't get its own operations to agree on a single number. Today it runs the same job for teams at over a thousand companies.

Our story

Every tool told a different story about the same process.

In 2019, our founding team was running operations for a mid-size logistics company, stitching together spreadsheets, a ticketing tool, and three approval chains that never quite agreed with each other.

Fulcrum began as the internal fix. We open-sourced the idea, then the product — and it turned out almost every enterprise operations team had built the same duct-taped system.

What drives us

Mission, vision, and how we get there.

Mission

Give every enterprise team one place to run, trust and improve their operations — without six tabs open at once.

Vision

A world where "the system is down" never stalls a business decision, because the system is the source of truth.

Approach

Ship with the customers who feel the problem most acutely, then generalize — never the other way around.

How we work

Values we actually hire and fire on.

01

Default to the operator's view

If a decision doesn't hold up from the perspective of the person running the process daily, it doesn't ship.

02

Write it down, make it legible

Undocumented process is just tribal knowledge waiting to break when someone leaves.

03

Slow is smooth, smooth is fast

We'd rather ship a rollout that holds than a demo that impresses and breaks in week two.

04

Boring technology, bold outcomes

We save our creativity for the customer's problem, not for reinventing infrastructure.
